Metric guide:
  • Occlusion Spearman ρ — Rank correlation between attribution magnitude and component occlusion effects; higher means stronger agreement with this proxy, not proof of explanation correctness.
  • Deletion Prob. Drop — Change in predicted-class probability after removing top-ranked URL components. Larger drops show greater sensitivity to this deletion procedure, not necessarily more faithful explanations.
  • Cosine Similarity — Cosine similarity of component attribution vectors under controlled URL modifications (higher = more similar).
  • Avg. Runtime — Mean wall-clock seconds to generate one explanation (lower = faster).
  • Top-Component Stability — Fraction of tested modifications retaining the same top-ranked URL component (higher = more consistent).

The graphs summarize offline evaluation, not the currently entered URL. Methods may use different attribution scales and masking assumptions. Results are descriptive, not a statistical significance test.

⚠ Experimental AI system. Predictions are not verified website safety assessments. Do not use as the sole basis for security decisions.
